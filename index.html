Student Tracking, AI Resolution and Sanction System

NC-STARS provides a highly secure, centralized digital ecosystem for comprehensive student discipline and welfare management.

  • Real-time tracking of student infractions
  • AI-assisted sanction recommendations

Contextual AI Engine

The system automatically analyzes the student's history and cross-references it against the NC Student Handbook (Section M.4) to provide an objective and fair disciplinary recommendation.

Student Handbook

Section M.4: Classifications & Actions

Level Disciplinary Action Description
L1 Verbal Warning The student is reprimanded for unacceptable behavior.
L2 Written Reprimand A formal written statement documenting that a violation of specific regulations has occurred.
- Written Apology A formal letter of apology addressed to the aggrieved party or institution (Supplementary).
- Counseling / Mediation Intervention focused on addressing root causes, retaliation, or conflict resolution (Supplementary).
L3 Community Service 15–20 hours of service around the school (students may still attend classes).
L4 Suspension Exclusion from classes and campus activities for 5 to 20 school days (not exceeding 20% of term). Carries forfeiture of right to make up missed quizzes/homework.
L5 Dropping/Dismissal The student may be dropped during the school year. Transfer credentials will be issued immediately.
L6 Expulsion Permanent removal from Norzagaray College.

Major Violations

  • 1. Fraternities and Sororities

    Recruitment / Membership, Conducting Hazing / Initiations, Wearing/Displaying Org Symbols, Threatening Students to Join.

    L4 / L5 / L6
  • 2. Bullying/Harassment

    Verbal Assault, Physical Bullying, Social Isolation / Manipulation, Cyberbullying, Catcalling / Gender-based Slurs.

    1ST: L2/L3 2ND: L3/L4 3RD: L4/L5
  • 3. Sexual Harassment

    Catcalling / Sexist Slurs, Unwanted Physical Contact, Stalking / Online Harassment, Requests for Sexual Favors.

    1ST: L3/L4/L5/L6 2ND: L4/L5/L6
  • 4. Dangerous Drugs

    Possession, Use/Administration, Entering Campus Intoxicated/Under Influence, Distribution/Selling.

    L5 / L6
  • 5. Vandalism

    Defacing Property, Destruction of Equipment, Tampering with Electricals/AC, Vandalism of Bulletins.

    1ST: L3 2ND: L4 3RD: L5
  • 6. Public Display of Affection (PDA)

    Intimate necking, petting, kissing, Acts of Immorality / Pornography, Indecent Civilian Attire.

    L5
  • 7. Cheating

    Possession of Notes, Copying, Proxy Exam Takers, Unauthorized Communication, Plagiarism.

    1ST: L4 2ND: L5

Minor/Behavioral Violations

  • 1. Uniform & Grooming

    Failure to wear prescribed uniform, prohibited attire, No School ID, Piercings/Accessories, Hair Coloring, Earrings (Male).

    1ST: L1 2ND: L2 3RD: L3
  • 2. Classroom Disruptions

    Sleeping, Cellphones/gadgets, leaving without permission, Inappropriate Language, Disturbing Class, Sitting on Tables, Running.

    1ST: L1 2ND: L2 3RD: L3
  • 3. Facility Misuse

    Sitting on tables, failing to turn off lights/fans, Littering, Unauthorized Use, Tampering Notices, CR Negligence.

    1ST: L1 2ND: L2 3RD: L3
  • 4. Attendance & Punctuality

    Tardiness, Unauthorized absences, Leaving Class Without Permission, No Uniform/ID Entry Violation.

    1ST: L1 2ND: L2 3RD: L3

Application Note

Disciplinary actions shall be imposed commensurate to the degree of gravity of the offense committed. Mitigating or aggravating circumstances shall be considered in determining the appropriate penalty.
